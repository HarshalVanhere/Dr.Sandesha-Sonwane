Physiotherapy Services in Nashik

Dr. Sandesha Sonawane provides clinic-grade, one-on-one home physiotherapy in Nashik for the conditions below. Every session is delivered at your home with portable clinical equipment — no travel required.

Back Pain & Sciatica Physiotherapy

Decompression, postural correction, core activation, and pain-management therapy for slipped discs, muscle spasms, and spinal stenosis.

Learn more

Neck & Shoulder Pain Physiotherapy

Relief from cervical spondylosis pain, frozen shoulder stiffness, and postural headaches using manual mobilization, stretching, and dry needling.

Learn more

Knee Pain & Arthritis Physiotherapy

Cartilage unloading exercises, joint mobilization, and quadriceps strengthening to manage Knee Osteoarthritis and delay surgical intervention.

Learn more

Sports Injury Rehabilitation

Structured rehab for ACL tears, ankle sprains, tennis elbow, and rotator cuff injuries to safely return to full performance.

Learn more

Post-Surgery Rehabilitation

Structured recovery plans after Total Knee Replacement (TKR), Total Hip Replacement (THR), ligament reconstructions, and fracture surgeries.

Learn more

Elderly Care Physiotherapy

Balance training, fall prevention protocols, and mobility assistance for senior citizens to maintain active independence.

Learn more
