Knee Pain & Arthritis Physiotherapy in Nashik

Home-based physiotherapy with cartilage unloading exercises, joint mobilization, and quadriceps strengthening to manage Knee Osteoarthritis and delay surgical intervention.

Understanding Knee Pain & Arthritis

Knee Osteoarthritis is a common cause of chronic knee pain, stiffness, and reduced mobility, particularly affecting weight-bearing activities like walking and climbing stairs. Physiotherapy plays a central role in non-surgical management by strengthening the muscles that support the joint and improving overall joint mechanics.

Dr. Sandesha Sonawane assesses joint stability, range of motion, and pain patterns at your first home visit to build a plan appropriate to your stage of arthritis.

Who May Benefit

  • Patients diagnosed with Knee Osteoarthritis
  • Those experiencing knee stiffness or reduced walking tolerance
  • Patients exploring non-surgical management before considering knee replacement
  • Anyone who finds it difficult to travel to a clinic due to knee pain

Our Treatment Approach

Treatment focuses on cartilage unloading exercises, joint mobilization, and progressive quadriceps strengthening to improve joint support and reduce load on the knee. Every plan follows the same structured process:

  • Clinical Assessment — evaluating joint stability, muscle strength, and range of motion
  • Custom Treatment Plan — a recovery roadmap suited to your stage of knee pain or arthritis
  • Interactive Home Visits — hands-on mobilization and supervised strengthening exercises
  • Recovery Monitoring — tracking mobility and strength gains over time

What to Expect at Home

Sessions run about 45 to 60 minutes and include an assessment, hands-on joint mobilization, and supervised strengthening exercises. Portable modalities such as TENS and heating pads are brought to your home where needed. A prescription is helpful but not mandatory to begin.
