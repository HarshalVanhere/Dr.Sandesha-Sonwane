Back Pain & Sciatica Physiotherapy in Nashik

Home-based physiotherapy for back pain and sciatica — decompression-focused exercises, postural correction, core activation, and pain-management therapy for slipped discs, muscle spasms, and spinal stenosis.

Understanding Back Pain & Sciatica

Lower back pain and sciatica are among the most common reasons patients in Nashik seek physiotherapy. Sciatica — pain that radiates from the lower back down the leg due to nerve irritation — is often linked to a slipped (herniated) disc, muscle spasm, or spinal stenosis. Left unmanaged, these conditions can restrict daily movement and make simple tasks like sitting, standing, or walking uncomfortable.

Dr. Sandesha Sonawane assesses your movement pattern, posture, and pain triggers during the first home visit, then builds a treatment plan around your specific presentation rather than a generic protocol.

Who May Benefit

  • Patients with lower back pain from muscle spasm or poor posture
  • Sciatica sufferers with radiating leg pain or numbness
  • Diagnosed cases of slipped disc or spinal stenosis seeking non-surgical management
  • Patients who find travelling to a clinic painful or difficult

Our Treatment Approach

Treatment combines manual therapy, targeted core-activation exercises, postural correction, and electrotherapy modalities such as TENS where clinically appropriate. Sessions follow the same four-step process used across all home visits:

  • Clinical Assessment — evaluating range of motion, muscle strength, and nerve involvement at home
  • Custom Treatment Plan — a recovery roadmap built around your specific back or sciatic pain pattern
  • Interactive Home Visits — hands-on therapy and supervised exercises using portable equipment
  • Recovery Monitoring — tracking progress and adjusting intensity as pain reduces

What to Expect at Home

Each session runs approximately 45 to 60 minutes and includes an assessment check, hands-on treatment, electrotherapy where needed, and supervised therapeutic exercises. All equipment — TENS units, exercise bands, and other therapeutic gear — is brought to your home; you don't need to arrange anything. A doctor's prescription is helpful but not mandatory to begin.
