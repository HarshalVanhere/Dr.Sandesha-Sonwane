Dr. Sandesha Sonawane (Physiotherapist)

As a licensed physiotherapist registered with the Maharashtra State Council of Occupational Therapy , I specialize in providing personalized, evidence-based physical therapy services.

My clinical philosophy centers on restoring functionality and movement by combining manual therapy, electrotherapy, and customized exercise therapy. Home-based care ensures that patients recover in their natural environment, reducing hospital-acquired infection risks and scheduling stresses.

How It Works

Our Home Physiotherapy Process

A structured, four-step clinical pathway designed to transition you from acute pain to optimal physical function safely.

1

Clinical Assessment

First, we conduct a comprehensive evaluation of range of motion, muscle strength, joint stability, and neurological reflexes in the comfort of your home.

2

Custom Treatment Plan

Based on the assessment, we map out a clinical roadmap with clear recovery timelines, selecting custom exercises and active manual therapies.

3

Interactive Home Visits

We visit your home at scheduled slots, bringing portable medical devices (like TENS/ultrasound) to facilitate hands-on recovery and exercise supervision.

4

Recovery Monitoring

We track progress using standardized clinical metrics, adjust treatment intensities dynamically, and provide transition programs for long-term fitness.
